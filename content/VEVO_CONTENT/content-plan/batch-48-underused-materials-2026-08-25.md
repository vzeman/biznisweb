# VEVO Batch 48: Underused Materials

Date: 2026-08-25
Cluster: C09A extension, expert material encyclopedias
Planned size: 4 articles

## Selection boundary

The batch covers four materially different reader problems: a bast fibre, a regenerated cellulosic fibre, a woollen woven cloth and a heavy plain-weave construction. The articles must not collapse into generic washing advice. Each one needs its own mechanism, home diagnosis, garment examples, care limits and source set.

Excluded from this batch:

- taffeta and jacquard because the exploratory title guard returned review;
- lame and brocade because they would sit too close to the published Lurex article without a separate metallic-textile comparison;
- sherpa because the current fleece and boucle coverage already handles closely related surface problems;
- gabardine remains excluded because it belongs to the twill family already covered by the keper guide.

## Article briefs

### Čo je ramia: pevné rastlinné vlákno, krčivosť a pranie

- Separate ramie fibre from linen, cotton, hemp and fabrics merely described as linen-like.
- Explain bast origin, degumming, stiffness, lustre, low elasticity, wrinkles and blend behaviour.
- Cover ramie shirts, dresses, table textiles and knit blends separately.
- Include a practical wet-care workflow, shrinkage diagnosis, ironing and storage.
- Use EU fibre labelling, peer-reviewed ramie laundering and fibre-property research, AATCC and GINETEX sources.

### Čo je cupro: regenerované celulózové vlákno, splývavosť a starostlivosť

- Explain that cupro is regenerated cellulose made by the cuprammonium process, not silk and not ordinary cotton.
- Separate the generic fibre name cupro from the Bemberg trademark and from viscose, modal and lyocell.
- Cover lining, dresses, blouses, moisture handling, static, colour, wet weight and fibrillation risk.
- Distinguish home-washable cupro from dry-clean-only tailored garments.
- Use EU fibre labelling, current Asahi Kasei technical information, AATCC and GINETEX sources.

### Čo je tvíd: hrubšia vlnená tkanina, žmolky a čistenie

- Explain tweed as a fabric family rather than a fibre label.
- Distinguish generic tweed, Harris Tweed, herringbone pattern and knitted tweed-look products.
- Cover brushing, airing, spot treatment, tailored-jacket construction, pilling, moth prevention and storage.
- State clearly that a wool face does not override lining, interfacing, shoulder structure or the care label.
- Use Harris Tweed Authority, Woolmark, ASTM, EU fibre labelling and GINETEX sources.

### Čo je canvas: pevné plátno, škvrny a správne pranie

- Explain canvas as a heavy plain-weave or related construction, not one fibre.
- Separate cotton canvas, polyester canvas, duck, coated/waxed canvas and canvas-look footwear.
- Cover bags, shoes, aprons, workwear, covers and home textiles with different immersion limits.
- Explain abrasion, tear propagation, coatings, colour transfer, oil stains and dimensional change.
- Use CottonWorks weaving material, ASTM abrasion/tear standards, AATCC soil-release testing, EU fibre labelling and GINETEX.
