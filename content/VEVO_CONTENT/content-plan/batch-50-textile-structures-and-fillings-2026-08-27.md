## Purpose

This batch extends the material encyclopaedia without creating a synonym of an existing article. It covers four different material systems: a ribbed woven fabric, a high-loft filling, a knitted spacer structure and a lightweight plain-weave fabric. The practical advice is bounded by the care label and the construction of the whole product.

## Articles

1. `co-je-rips-priecne-rebrovana-tkanina-oder-a-spravna-starostlivost`
   - Distinguish woven rips/repp from knitted rib and corduroy.
   - Explain rib direction, abrasion on ridges, seam behaviour, pressing and cleaning by fibre content.
2. `co-je-vatelin-objemova-vypln-zliehanie-a-bezpecne-pranie`
   - Distinguish batting from loose fill, foam and fusible interlining.
   - Explain loft, migration, quilting density, compression recovery and the whole-product care boundary.
3. `co-je-3d-sietovina-distancny-uplet-prudenie-vzduchu-a-pranie`
   - Distinguish spacer knit from ordinary mesh, foam laminate and rigid plastic grid.
   - Explain two faces, connecting yarns, air flow, compression, trapped grit and drying through the full thickness.
4. `co-je-batist-jemna-platnova-tkanina-priesvitnost-a-pranie`
   - Distinguish batiste from voile, lawn, muslin and organza without treating the name as a fibre.
   - Explain fine plain weave, seam slippage, snagging, transparency, stain treatment and low-pressure pressing.
