# VEVO Batch 49: Household Material Systems

Date: 2026-08-27
Cluster: C09A extension, expert materials and household-textile systems
Planned size: 4 articles

## Selection boundary

The batch covers four different systems rather than four interchangeable fabrics: a patterned woven construction, a hollow plant filling, a stiff leaf fibre used in rugs, and an internal garment support. Each article must identify the material correctly, separate fibre from construction, explain the dominant failure mechanism and tell the reader when ordinary laundering is not appropriate.

Excluded from this batch:

- the broad nonwoven title because the duplicate guard blocked the proposed wording;
- cork textile because the title guard returned review;
- coconut fibre because the practical intent sits close to the existing entrance-mat guide;
- vatelin, felt and 3D mesh because they need their own comparison-led batches;
- gabardine, taffeta, jacquard, lame, brocade and sherpa remain deferred under the batch 48 decision record.

## Article briefs

### Čo je damask: obojstranný tkaný vzor a starostlivosť o obrusy a obliečky

- Explain damask as a figured weave, not a fibre and not merely a printed ornament.
- Separate single-colour satin or twill effects from brocade, jacquard as a production system and printed damask-look products.
- Cover tablecloths, napkins, bedding, curtains and historical silk damask separately.
- Diagnose pulled floats, shine change, food stains, candle wax, dimensional change and ironing marks.
- Use CottonWorks weaving material, museum textile evidence, AATCC methods, GINETEX and EU fibre-labelling rules.

### Čo je kapok: ľahká rastlinná výplň, vlhkosť a starostlivosť

- Define kapok as fruit fibre from Ceiba pentandra and explain the hollow lumen and waxy surface.
- Separate loose kapok filling from kapok-blend batting, removable covers and marketing names.
- Cover pillows, cushions, meditation products and historical buoyancy uses without inferring one wash method.
- Diagnose fill migration, lumps, odour, incomplete drying and loss of loft.
- Use EU fibre-labelling rules, peer-reviewed kapok structure reviews, AATCC, GINETEX and verified VEVO bedding guides.

### Čo je sisal: pevné listové vlákno, škvrny od vody a čistenie kobercov

- Define sisal as Agave sisalana leaf fibre and separate it from jute, seagrass, coir and synthetic sisal-look rugs.
- Explain why fibre stiffness, backing, dye and edge binding control cleaning.
- Cover dry soil, liquid spills, water rings, browning, pet accidents and drying with low-moisture boundaries.
- State clearly that a generic carpet-cleaning instruction does not override the rug maker's label.
- Use EU fibre-labelling rules, FAO sisal material, carpet-care standards, AATCC and verified VEVO rug guides.

### Čo je vlizelín: výstuž odevu, lepidlo a bezpečné čistenie

- Explain the Slovak common term while distinguishing interlining function from one fibre or one manufacturing method.
- Separate fusible and sew-in products and nonwoven, woven and knitted carriers.
- Cover collars, cuffs, waistbands, facings, embroidery backings and tailored fronts.
- Diagnose bubbling, delamination, adhesive strike-through, shrinkage mismatch and distorted pressing.
- Use current ISO nonwoven vocabulary, Freudenberg/Vlieseline technical material, ASTM/AATCC methods, GINETEX and verified VEVO tailored-garment guides.

## Shared publication requirements

- Current date is allowed; exact clean slugs are mandatory and existing URLs must not change.
- Minimum 2,800 visible words per article, starting with a direct lay answer and continuing into technical depth.
- Two responsive tables, at least ten styled blocks and two visible action buttons per article.
- One concrete VEVO product card and one relevant VEVO category card without a fixed price.
- Product copy must state the material boundary; it may not imply that a washable cover makes a filling, adhesive, rug backing or tailored structure washable.
- Only verified VEVO destinations and current authoritative or primary technical sources.
- No internal workflow vocabulary in public text.
- Hidden-first API creation, exact-title/slug readback, independent public verification and mobile layout QA.
