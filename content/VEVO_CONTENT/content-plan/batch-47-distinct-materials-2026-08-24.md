## Duplicate decision

The final titles returned `ok` against 839 merged live Blog, FAQ, glossary, RSS, and local records. Tulle was excluded because VEVO already has a detailed guide to washing tulle skirts and veils. Organza and tulle are not drafted in the same batch. Gabardine remains excluded because it is too close to the recently published twill construction guide. Lurex is treated as a yarn/material guide, not as a duplicate of lamé or brocade fabric.

- Use only verified VEVO article, category, product, and authoritative external links.
- Include one concrete product card and one category card without a fixed price.
