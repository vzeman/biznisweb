# VEVO batch 52: distinct textile systems

Date: 2026-08-27
Planned section: Blog, SK language, page 309, News block 765

## Purpose

This batch deliberately mixes four different decision systems instead of repeating one material template: a shirting weave, a feather-retaining bedding shell, a leaf fibre used in rigid and decorative products, and a dense cotton fabric with a raised face. Each article starts with a simple answer and then explains the construction that changes cleaning, drying, repair and purchase decisions.

## Articles

1. `co-je-oxfordska-tkanina-koselova-vazba-golier-a-spravne-pranie`
   - Explain the usual 2 x 1 basket-derived shirting weave and the basic, pinpoint and royal Oxford boundary.
   - Separate cotton shirting from coated polyester products sold under the same broad retail term.
   - Cover collar and cuff soil, seam movement, shrinkage, drying and ironing.
2. `co-je-sypkovina-husta-tkanina-na-perie-skvrny-a-pranie`
   - Explain why a dense shell, seams and finishing retain down or feather filling.
   - Separate a protruding quill, escaped fibre, open seam and structurally worn shell.
   - Cover label reading, air permeability, drying, repairs and professional re-filling without duplicating a feather-pillow washing guide.
3. `co-je-abaka-pevne-listove-vlakno-vlhkost-a-starostlivost`
   - Explain the leaf-sheath origin of abaca, official grading and the sinamay textile boundary.
   - Separate fibre-level strength and salt-water resistance from the care limits of dyes, starch, glue, linings and shaped objects.
   - Cover dust, spot cleaning, water rings, drying and storage of hats, baskets, mats and decorative textiles.
4. `co-je-moleskin-husta-brusena-bavlna-lesk-a-spravne-pranie`
   - Explain dense weft-faced cotton construction and mechanical sueding, napping, shearing or brushing.
   - Separate textile moleskin from animal skin and adhesive medical products.
   - Cover trouser stains, nap direction, polishing, lint, shrinkage, drying and low-pressure ironing.
