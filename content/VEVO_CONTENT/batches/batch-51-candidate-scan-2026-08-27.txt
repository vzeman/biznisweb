Selected:
1. Čo je chambray: farebná osnova, svetlý útok a správne pranie
   - Distinct from the existing denim, poplin and plain-weave guides.
   - The article must explain yarn-dyed colour mixing, the plain-weave boundary, collar/cuff soil, fading and shirt care.
2. Čo je matelassé: plastický tkaný reliéf, zrážanie a starostlivosť
   - Distinct from the existing damask, jacquard, quilted-fill and bedding guides.
   - The article must distinguish a woven relief from sewn quilting, padding and printed imitation.
3. Čo je ženilka: vlasová priadza, uvoľňovanie chĺpkov a čistenie
   - Distinct from the existing bouclé, velvet, velour and pilling guides.
   - The article must distinguish loose surface fibre, pile loss, a pulled yarn and a structural break across garments, blankets and upholstery.
4. Čo je panamová väzba: košíková tkanina, posun nití a pranie
   - Distinct from the existing plain weave, Oxford, canvas and ripstop coverage.
   - The article must explain grouped yarns, lower stability than simple plain weave, seam slippage and the retail use of the term hopsack.
