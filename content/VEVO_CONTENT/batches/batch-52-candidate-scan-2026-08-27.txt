Selected:
1. Čo je oxfordská tkanina: košeľová väzba, golier a správne pranie
   - Distinct from the existing poplin, chambray and panama-weave articles.
   - The article must explain 2 x 1 Oxford weave, basic versus pinpoint Oxford, yarn-dyed colour, collar/cuff soil and the separate coated-polyester retail meaning used for bags.
2. Čo je sypkovina: prečo uniká perie, kontrola švov a bezpečné čistenie
   - Distinct from the existing guides for washing a feather pillow, a duvet and synthetic filling.
   - The article must focus on the casing system: downproofness, air permeability, seams, quill holes, leakage diagnosis and the boundary between cleaning a surface and opening or replacing the shell.
3. Čo je abaka: pevné listové vlákno, vlhkosť a starostlivosť
   - Distinct from the existing sisal, ramie and kapok articles.
   - The article must distinguish Musa textilis from hemp and ordinary banana marketing, explain sinamay and rigid home accessories, and avoid promising that salt-water resistance makes a finished item washable.
4. Čo je moleskin: hustá brúsená bavlna, lesk a správne pranie
   - Distinct from flannel, corduroy, velvet, canvas and ordinary cotton guides.
   - The article must distinguish dense weft-faced construction and a mechanically raised or suede-like face from animal skin and adhesive medical moleskin, then cover nap direction, polishing, lint and trouser care.
