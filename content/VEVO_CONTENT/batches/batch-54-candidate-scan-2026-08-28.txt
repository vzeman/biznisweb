Selected:
1. Čo je buckram: vystužená tkanina, tvarovanie, prach a bezpečné čistenie
   - A sized or coated supporting fabric used in interlinings, hats, books and shaped objects, not another ordinary canvas or washable-cotton guide.
   - The article must explain why water can soften, move or mark the sizing; separate removable washable interfacing from built-in support; and prefer dry soil removal or specialist care when the whole object cannot be washed.
2. Čo je madras: károvaná košeľovina, púšťanie farby a správne pranie
   - A yarn-dyed lightweight cotton check with a separate historical "bleeding Madras" variant, not a generic plaid or all-purpose colour-transfer article.
   - The article must explain that not every modern madras is designed to bleed, cover first-wash isolation, wet crocking, mixed light/dark checks, shrinkage, patchwork and when persistent transfer is grounds for complaint.
3. Čo je šantán: nepravidelný povrch, hodvábne a zmesové varianty
   - A plain-weave fabric whose irregular filling yarn creates deliberate slubs, distinct from the crisp cross-rib and uniform surface of taft.
   - The article must separate a design slub from a snag, broken yarn or pulled seam; distinguish silk and synthetic variants; and cover water marks, pressing, structured garments and professional-care boundaries.
4. Čo je challis: mäkká splývavá tkanina, zrážanie a pranie
   - A soft lightweight plain-weave construction made from wool, cotton, rayon/viscose or other staple fibres, not a synonym for viscose.
   - The article must cover drape, grain distortion, seam slippage, wet-weight support, shrinkage, pressing and why the fibre label overrides the trade name.
